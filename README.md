# Causal Access: A Decision-Theoretic Foundation for Representation, Selection, Report, and Agency in the Science of Consciousness

By Lakshmi Krishna G.

**Read it:** [web version](https://meta-lucky-builds.github.io/attempt-at-the-hard-problem/) · [PDF](Attempt-at-the-hard-problem.pdf)

This paper is an attempt at the hard problem of consciousness, and it does not solve it. It proposes a foundation for the
functional part of the problem. Access is defined as causal use by independently specified readers, a workspace is
separated from a mere relay by selection, and resource limits are modeled as constrained decision problems. Twenty
propositions and three corollaries, most of them elementary, are used to work through fifty problems from psychophysics,
neurology, psychiatry, philosophy and machine learning. The paper grades how far it explains each one, including the
ones it cannot explain, such as why any organization feels.

## Status

- No empirical test of the framework has been run, and none of the worked accounts should be read as confirmed.
- The paper has not been peer reviewed. Its adversarial review so far has been by AI systems only.
- Appendix D describes two numerical-check scripts, `v7_recheck.py` and `paper_checks.py`. They are not yet in this
  repository.
- References marked † in the paper were cited from recall and have not been confirmed by a search.
- Claude was used in this work, including in reviewing and helping execute tests. The author holds the responsibility
  for it.

## Related work

- [A Control-Theoretic Formulation of Global Workspace Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15926) (Kanai, 16 Aug 2026) is another formal treatment of the global workspace.
- [Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15495) (Gurnee et al., 16 Jul 2026) tests workspace claims with causal interventions; the paper cites it.
- [From cacophony to hierarchy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35618) (Chandaria et al., 28 Sep 2026) is a framework for assessing AI consciousness that sets the hard problem aside.
